# Stage 2 data-source inventory

Checked 2026-09-12. This inventory supports a bounded aggregate pilot; it does
not establish that the original quote-level hypotheses are estimable. The
machine-readable companion is [source_matrix.csv](source_matrix.csv).

## Decision

Use BTS DB1B Market as the fare/traffic source for 2023-2024, joined to DB1B
Ticket only for defensible itinerary filters. Aggregate to directional
airport-pair x carrier x quarter. The candidate sample is approximately twelve
directional markets linking the hub airports ORD, DEN, and DFW with ATL, LAX,
JFK, and SEA. Freeze the exact routes and any minimum-cell rule before examining
outcomes.

Use a strictly lagged, same-quarter climatological weather-risk score constructed
from Open-Meteo historical daily weather at the seven airports. Treat that score
as a retrospective proxy for information that could have been known before the
quarter. Validate it against a separate BTS carrier-airport-month operational
outcomes file. This is an aggregate redesign, not a reconstruction of quotes or
forecast vintages.

## Source roles

| Source | Role | Resolution and coverage | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [DB1B Market](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/Fields.asp?gnoyr_VQ=FHK) | Fare and observed passenger quantity | Ticket-market rows; quarterly 10% sample through 2025 Q2 | Primary |
| DB1B Ticket | Itinerary-level screening fields | Ticket rows; quarterly through 2025 Q2 | Acquire for filters; do not treat as quotes |
| [O&D DB1C / O&D40](https://www.bts.gov/OD-40) | DB1B successor | Monthly 40% sample from July 2025; changed reporting carrier and fields | Future extension only; do not pool without a bridge design |
| [T-100 Domestic Segment](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/DatabaseInfo.asp?QO_VQ=EEE&Yv0x=D) | Capacity and traffic robustness controls | Carrier-route-month passengers, seats, departures and load factor | Optional robustness/control |
| [BTS Delay Causes](https://transtats.bts.gov/OT_Delay/ot_delaycause1.asp) | External validation of operational risk | Carrier-airport-month outcomes from June 2003; marketing/reporting scope changes in 2018 | Primary validation source only |
| [Open-Meteo Historical Weather](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api) | Historical daily weather used to form lagged climatological risk | Reanalysis/modelled weather; global point API | Primary risk input, with retrospective-vintage label |
| [Open-Meteo Previous Runs](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/previous-runs-api) | True fixed-lead forecast sensitivity | Most variables from January 2024; 1-7 day lead fields | Useful 2024 validation, not alignable to quarterly DB1B purchase time |
| [NOAA/NCEI model archive](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/weather-climate-models) | Government-hosted forecast-vintage fallback | Historical numerical weather prediction archives; high-volume GRIB access | Technically valid but disproportionate for this pilot |

## Access tests and exact endpoints

The existing manifests establish full downloads and ZIP integrity for DB1B
Market 2024 Q1 and Q2. On 2026-09-12, anonymous HEAD requests also returned 200
for DB1B Market 2025 Q2 (110,296,405 bytes) and 404 for 2025 Q3. That is
consistent with BTS's statement that DB1B collection ended in July 2025 and
DB1C began then, rather than evidence of a transient missing quarter. See the
[official O&D transition page](https://www.bts.gov/topics/airlines-and-airports/origin-and-destination-survey-data).

The preferred small operational validation download was generated by POSTing
the following ordinary form fields to
`https://transtats.bts.gov/OT_Delay/ot_delaycause1.asp?20=E`:

```text
Carrier=All
Airport=All31
PeriodFrom=24265
PeriodTo=24300
```

Those period values mean January 2022 and December 2024. Extract the `Download
Raw Data` link from the returned page rather than hard-coding its obfuscated
query. The anonymous download returned HTTP 200 and a 504,707-byte ZIP. In-memory
inspection found `Airline_Delay_Cause.csv` (2,329,091 uncompressed bytes),
13,252 data rows, exactly 2022-2024, all 31 major airports, and all seven pilot
airports. Its fields include flights, delays, cause counts/minutes,
cancellations, and diversions. The ZIP also contains the BTS column-definition
workbook. Its definitions label `arr_flights` "Arrival Flights,"
`arr_cancelled` "flight cancelled," and `weather_ct` "Weather Count for airline
cause of delay." Use `arr_cancelled / arr_flights` as the cancellation-rate
validation outcome after checking denominators in every aggregate. `weather_ct`
is a prorated attribution among delayed flights, not a count of weather-caused
cancellations. No persistent raw file was created by this investigation. The
exact verified request metadata are in
[delay_causes_request.json](delay_causes_request.json).

For a stable but much larger fallback, this official January 2024 marketing-
carrier ASQP archive returned HTTP 200 and 31,090,329 bytes:

```text
https://transtats.bts.gov/PREZIP/On_Time_Marketing_Carrier_On_Time_Performance_Beginning_January_2018_2024_1.zip
```

The [official ASQP table description](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/Tables.asp?QO_VQ=EFD)
confirms flight-level schedules, actual performance, cancellations, diversions,
and delay causes. Prefer the small aggregate file unless flight-level validation
becomes essential.

Three no-key Open-Meteo requests for ORD on 2024-01-15 returned 24 hourly rows:
Historical Forecast, Previous Runs with three-day-lead fields, and the archive
reanalysis endpoint. These were access/schema tests only. The archive test URL
was:

```text
https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive?latitude=41.9742&longitude=-87.9073&start_date=2024-01-15&end_date=2024-01-15&hourly=temperature_2m,precipitation,wind_gusts_10m&timezone=UTC
```

## Recorded failures

- Direct HEAD requests to three legacy BTS ASQP attachment URLs linked from the
  official monthly download page returned 403. Equivalent TranStats PREZIP
  archives returned 200, so use PREZIP or a normal browser GET if needed.
- Guessed T-100 PREZIP names returned 404. Use the TranStats download form or
  the official Data Bank 28 products rather than inventing filenames.
- DB1B 2025 Q3 returned 404 because the quarterly series stops after Q2; use
  DB1C for July 2025 onward and document the structural break.
