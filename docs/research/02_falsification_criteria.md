# Falsification, sensitivity, and stopping criteria

Specified at Stage 0 before estimates.

1. Original hypotheses: declare not identified if fare observation times and
   matching information sets cannot be recovered. Do not substitute outcomes
   or retrospective archive values and keep the original causal interpretation.
2. Baseline diagnostic: if traffic-price slope is zero/negative in benign conditions
   or changes sign under route/carrier and calendar controls, report descriptive
   sign instability. Equilibrium quantity alone cannot validate demand pressure.
   No automatic model changes to obtain a positive sign.
3. Aggregate signs: report estimates and two-sided 95% intervals for A1/A2. A
   wrong sign or interval crossing zero is contrary/inconclusive evidence, not
   support. Multiple exploratory specifications are not independent confirmations.
4. Risk support: require available prior-year weather coverage >=95% of calendar
   days in every airport-quarter used. Missing risk remains missing, never zero.
   Test strict temporal exclusion of target-year/future weather from climatology.
5. Precision: report observation and cluster counts, design rank and condition
   number. Do not report an absorbed or rank-deficient coefficient as identified.
   Few route clusters mean asymptotic intervals are approximate and fragile.
6. Sensitivity: compare unweighted cell OLS, passenger-weighted WLS, log fares,
   common/balanced support where available, and leave-one-hub-out interaction
   estimates. Report all attempted fits and failed/rank-deficient fits.
7. Product quality: audit fare plausibility, bulk fares, missing values, passengers,
   nonstop restriction, Ticket join multiplicity, and ticket dollar credibility.
   No outcome-driven trimming. Primary fare bounds USD 20-2,000; robustness
   USD 10-5,000 if source quality permits.
8. Do not interpret a weather index as operational risk unless operational
   validation is available. Report weather predictiveness separately from
   cancellation/delay validation; either may be weak or absent.
9. End recommendation A/B/C/D follows data and identification, not desired signs.
   A strong-paper label is unavailable solely from a small ecological pilot.

Kill the original empirical test for this pass if missing quote-time information
persists. Continue the explicitly narrower real-data pilot and report its limits,
as authorized. No synthetic historical fares or proprietary access substitutes.
